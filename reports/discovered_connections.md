## Review and evaluation

Use the application's Review tab or `python -m kgmap.cli export-reviews`, edit the JSON, then `import-reviews`. Review IDs persist across refreshes. Rejected extraction and concept-association edges are removed before visible scores are recomputed. Accepting a candidate records a mathematician's opinion; it does not turn similarity into a proven theorem. Corrected decisions require a note and preserve the original evidence. A scientific evaluation still needs a mathematician-labelled set: check author-cluster precision, topic extraction precision/recall, and connection usefulness, then compare against a shared-keywords-only baseline. No accuracy claim is made from software tests.
